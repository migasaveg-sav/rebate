Collections file

Used to calculate the payment status per SO. See the note about the matching logic below.

Matching works in two steps: 1) if the row carries an SO number in "Body Summary", the payment is assigned directly to that SO (exact match, the most reliable one); 2) for rows without that data, they are grouped by customer and distributed (FIFO, from the oldest SO to the most recent) against the outstanding balance of their included SOs. Rebate bracket table

version 1

Bracket scheme with surplus reset (non-progressive): each bracket has a single %. If the SO amount exceeds the last bracket, the surplus cycles back through the table from the start, in cycles, until it's used up.

Extra rebate on SOs paid 100%

In addition to the normal rebate (above), an additional rebate is calculated by applying a % directly on the amount of each SO with RAC/LCAC lines that has been collected 100%. It has no minimum amount and does not require the SO to be free of discounts — the only condition is full collection. This rebate does NOT accumulate between periods: it is only on what was collected 100% within each period. The % can change from one period to another (e.g. 2% in June, 1% in July) — it is set per period in step 1, next to each month. The value here is only the default % suggested for new periods that don't yet have their own.

Normal rebate (type 1) by customer — accumulated

The rebate is calculated once per customer, applying the brackets to the accumulated amount (previous periods + this period) of SOs validated as "Applies", paid 100% and without discounts. The amount per SO in the tables below is the proportional distribution of this total.

Extra rebate (type 2) by customer — this period only

Directly on SOs with RAC/LCAC lines paid 100% this period. No minimum, no validation requirement, no requirement to be free of discounts. Does not accumulate between periods.

Rebate by branch and customer

Sum of normal + extra rebate, grouped by branch, with each customer's subtotal within their branch.

Rebate detail by SO

Rebate already paid in previous period(s)

If this run combines the accumulated total of two or more periods and one of them was already paid before with this same tool, upload that export here to subtract it from the accumulated total and get the real amount pending for this period. The Rebate 1 and Rebate 2 columns (or "Rebate amount" / "Extra rebate paid on orders" if they come with those names) are taken from the file, grouped by Customer, and used against the Rebate 1 and Rebate 2 accumulated for each customer calculated in this run. Rows without a customer (for example, a totals row at the end of the file) are ignored.

Final result by customer

Rebate 1 Net = this run's accumulated Rebate 1 minus what was already paid last period — except when this run has no new accumulated Rebate 1 for a customer, in which case the historical amount is shown as-is instead of being subtracted, so this column never shows a negative number. Rebate 2 does not accumulate between periods, so its Historical column is for reference only — the Total column is the sum of Accumulated + Historical, not a subtraction. What's actually due now (Total Net Due) is Rebate 1 Net + Rebate 2 Accumulated (this period's own extra rebate only, since the historical Rebate 2 was already paid separately last period).
